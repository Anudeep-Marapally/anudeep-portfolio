01.

Which customers generate the highest sales value?

QUERY / ANALYSIS
=SUMIFS(Data[Total Sales],Data[Customer ID],A2)
INSIGHT

Customer-level aggregation identifies the highest-value buyers and creates a foundation for targeted retention or loyalty activity.

02.

Which product categories drive the strongest performance?

QUERY / ANALYSIS
=SUMIFS(Data[Total Sales],Data[Category],A2)
INSIGHT

Category performance shows where revenue is concentrated and where assortment decisions can have the largest commercial effect.

03.

Which products are the top revenue contributors?

QUERY / ANALYSIS
=SUMIFS(Data[Total Sales],Data[Product],A2)
INSIGHT

Top-product analysis helps distinguish broad category strength from the specific products responsible for sales.

04.

When are sales strongest across seasons?

QUERY / ANALYSIS
=SUMIFS(Data[Total Sales],Data[Season],A2)
INSIGHT

Seasonal analysis identifies demand cycles that can support inventory and promotional planning.

05.

Which payment methods are most used and valuable?

QUERY / ANALYSIS
=COUNTIF(Data[Payment Method],A2)
INSIGHT

Payment-method mix shows customer preference and helps identify the checkout methods that matter most to transaction volume.

06.

How do online and in-store channels compare?

QUERY / ANALYSIS
=SUMIFS(Data[Total Sales],Data[Sales Channel],A2)
INSIGHT

Comparing channels shows whether performance is concentrated online or in-store and provides a basis for channel-specific decisions.

07.

What should the retailer prioritize after cleaning the data?

QUERY / ANALYSIS
=RANK.EQ(B2,$B$2:$B$20,0)
INSIGHT

The final story combines customer, category, product, seasonal and channel findings instead of treating each Pivot Table as an isolated result.

OVERALL INSIGHTS

Overall Retail Insights

The cleaned retail dataset reveals where revenue is concentrated, when demand peaks and how customers behave across products, payments and sales channels.

Customer value is uneven

A relatively small set of customers contributes substantial sales value and can support targeted retention efforts.

Product and category mix drives performance

Category and top-product analysis identifies the parts of the assortment with the greatest commercial impact.

Seasonality should influence planning

Demand cycles provide useful signals for inventory and promotional timing.

Channel and payment behavior matter

Online/in-store and payment-method comparisons reveal where transaction activity is concentrated.

CONCLUSION

What the project tells us

The retail analysis demonstrates the value of moving from messy source data to a validated, decision-ready view of performance. Customer value, product mix, seasonality, payment behavior and sales channels each explain a different part of the business. Together, they provide a stronger basis for retention, assortment, inventory and channel decisions than any single Pivot Table or KPI.
