01.

Which customers generate the most revenue?

QUERY / ANALYSIS
SELECT C.CUSTOMER_ID, C.NAME AS CUSTOMER_NAME,
       ROUND(SUM(O.TOTAL_AMOUNT),2) AS REVENUE_GENERATED,
       ROUND(AVG(O.TOTAL_AMOUNT),2) AS AVERAGE_AMOUNT,
       COUNT(O.ORDER_ID) AS ORDER_COUNT
FROM ORDERS O
JOIN CUSTOMERS C ON C.CUSTOMER_ID = O.CUSTOMER_ID
WHERE O.STATUS = 'DELIVERED'
GROUP BY C.CUSTOMER_ID,C.NAME
ORDER BY REVENUE_GENERATED DESC
LIMIT 5;
INSIGHT

Customer 436 is the strongest revenue contributor in the repository analysis, combining high revenue with frequent ordering.

02.

What percentage of revenue is concentrated among the highest-value customers?

QUERY / ANALYSIS
WITH CUSTOMER_REVENUE AS (
 SELECT CUSTOMER_ID, SUM(TOTAL_AMOUNT) AS REVENUE
 FROM ORDERS
 WHERE STATUS='DELIVERED'
 GROUP BY CUSTOMER_ID
), TOP_CUSTOMERS AS (
 SELECT * FROM CUSTOMER_REVENUE
 ORDER BY REVENUE DESC
 LIMIT 10
)
SELECT ROUND(SUM(REVENUE)*100/(SELECT SUM(REVENUE) FROM CUSTOMER_REVENUE),2) AS TOP_CUSTOMER_SHARE
FROM TOP_CUSTOMERS;
INSIGHT

The project uses Pareto-style thinking to identify how much of the platform’s revenue depends on a small high-value customer group.

03.

Which customers have the strongest lifetime value?

QUERY / ANALYSIS
SELECT C.CUSTOMER_ID, C.NAME,
       COUNT(O.ORDER_ID) AS ORDER_COUNT,
       ROUND(SUM(O.TOTAL_AMOUNT),2) AS CUSTOMER_LIFETIME_VALUE
FROM CUSTOMERS C
JOIN ORDERS O ON C.CUSTOMER_ID=O.CUSTOMER_ID
WHERE O.STATUS='DELIVERED'
GROUP BY C.CUSTOMER_ID,C.NAME
ORDER BY CUSTOMER_LIFETIME_VALUE DESC
LIMIT 10;
INSIGHT

CLV highlights customers whose cumulative value is much higher than a single-order ranking would show, helping prioritize retention.

04.

How strongly are customers returning to the platform?

QUERY / ANALYSIS
SELECT CUSTOMER_ID, COUNT(ORDER_ID) AS ORDER_COUNT
FROM ORDERS
WHERE STATUS='DELIVERED'
GROUP BY CUSTOMER_ID
HAVING COUNT(ORDER_ID) > 1
ORDER BY ORDER_COUNT DESC;
INSIGHT

Repeat ordering is a direct signal of retention. The project uses it to identify customers who already show loyalty and customers who may need re-engagement.

05.

Which payment methods are most preferred?

QUERY / ANALYSIS
SELECT PAYMENT_MODE,
       COUNT(*) AS ORDERS_COUNT,
       ROUND(SUM(TOTAL_AMOUNT),2) AS REVENUE
FROM ORDERS
WHERE STATUS='DELIVERED'
GROUP BY PAYMENT_MODE
ORDER BY ORDERS_COUNT DESC;
INSIGHT

Payment preference reveals how customers complete purchases and where checkout experience should be optimized.

06.

How does delivery time affect customer satisfaction?

QUERY / ANALYSIS
SELECT
    CASE WHEN D.DELIVERY_TIME <= D.ESTIMATED_TIME THEN 'On / Under Estimate' ELSE 'Late' END AS DELIVERY_GROUP,
    ROUND(AVG(O.FEEDBACK_RATING),2) AS AVG_RATING,
    COUNT(*) AS ORDERS
FROM ORDERS O
JOIN DELIVERIES D ON O.ORDER_ID=D.ORDER_ID
WHERE O.STATUS='DELIVERED'
GROUP BY DELIVERY_GROUP;
INSIGHT

Delivery speed is analyzed against feedback rating because operational delays can directly affect customer satisfaction.

07.

Which restaurants combine revenue, ratings and delivery speed?

QUERY / ANALYSIS
SELECT R.RESTAURANT_ID,R.NAME,
       ROUND(SUM(O.TOTAL_AMOUNT),2) AS REVENUE,
       R.RATING, R.AVERAGE_DELIVERY_TIME
FROM RESTAURANTS R
JOIN ORDERS O ON R.RESTAURANT_ID=O.RESTAURANT_ID
WHERE R.IS_ACTIVE=1 AND O.STATUS='DELIVERED'
GROUP BY R.RESTAURANT_ID,R.NAME,R.RATING,R.AVERAGE_DELIVERY_TIME
ORDER BY REVENUE DESC
LIMIT 10;
INSIGHT

Restaurant success is multi-dimensional: high revenue is more meaningful when it is paired with strong ratings and efficient delivery.

OVERALL INSIGHTS

Overall Business Insights

Customer value, retention, delivery experience and restaurant performance are connected. The strongest opportunities come from retaining valuable customers while improving the operational experience that keeps them coming back.

High-value customers deserve focused retention

Revenue and CLV analysis show that customer value is uneven, so retention efforts should prioritize customers with strong current and future value.

Revenue concentration creates both opportunity and risk

A smaller high-value customer group contributes a meaningful share of platform revenue, making loyalty and re-engagement important.

Delivery experience influences satisfaction

Delivery speed should be treated as a customer-experience metric, not only an operational metric.

Restaurant success is multi-dimensional

The strongest restaurants are those that combine revenue with good ratings and efficient delivery rather than maximizing sales alone.

CONCLUSION

What the project tells us

The project demonstrates that food-delivery performance cannot be understood from revenue alone. The analysis connects customer value, repeat behavior, payment preference, delivery experience and restaurant quality into one decision framework. The clearest opportunity is to protect high-value customers, strengthen repeat ordering and address delivery friction, while recognizing restaurants that consistently deliver both commercial performance and customer satisfaction.
