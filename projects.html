Zara Sales & Revenue Analysis

View Project →

Skills & Tools

PythonPandasNumPyMatplotlibJupyter Notebook

Key Insights

  • Autumn and Winter generate the highest revenue, driven by seasonal demand.
  • Jackets are the top revenue-generating product category, while wool and cotton dominate overall revenue contribution.
  • The women's section consistently outperforms the men's section in revenue.
  • Aisle-positioned products generate higher revenue than end-cap and front-of-store placements.
  • Promotions are volume-driven rather than revenue-driven, and China remains the highest revenue-generating country after outlier treatment.
  • Revenue outlier treatment was applied only to revenue, preserving the original sales-volume and category distributions for demand analysis.
  • Autumn–Winter demand and strong performance from jackets, wool and cotton point to clear assortment-planning priorities.
